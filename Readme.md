# SaaS Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using **Power BI**, **PostgreSQL**, **SQL**, and **DAX** to analyze the performance of a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) company. The dashboard provides executives and business stakeholders with actionable insights into revenue, customers, subscriptions, and overall business performance.

---


# Project Overview

This project demonstrates how raw SaaS business data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using modern Business Intelligence tools.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), evaluate customer behavior, identify revenue trends, and analyze customer segmentation through interactive visualizations.

---

# Business Objectives

The dashboard answers key business questions such as:

- How much revenue is the business generating?
- How many active customers does the company have?
- What is the customer churn rate?
- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- Which industries contribute the most customers?
- How are new customers growing over time?
- What is the average revenue generated per customer (ARPU)?

# Dataset

The project uses a simulated SaaS business dataset stored in PostgreSQL.

- Subscription Plans
